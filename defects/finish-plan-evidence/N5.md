## What changed (smallest coherent change)

1. **Pacing** — `scripts/catchup-followup.ts` now paces sends to stay under the profile
   SMS per-minute cap. The cap is read the way the gate reads it
   (`config.comms.rate_caps.sms.per_minute`, default 5, matching `comms-rate-limiter`).
   Effective rate = `cap - 1` per rolling 60s (`effectivePerMinute`), lowered further by
   `--per-minute <n>` (override DOWNWARD only — clamped to `cap-1`, floored at 1). The loop
   sleeps `ceil(60000/perMinute)` ms between sends (not after the last) and prints
   `pacing: <n>/min`.

2. **Retryable, honest ledger** — chose the "do not leave a blocking ledger row" option,
   implemented in the catch-up send loop (contained; `hasAutomationRun` and other callers
   unchanged). A send that ends `blocked` with `gate_rule === 'rate-cap-exceeded'`, or
   `failed`, has its `automation_runs` row deleted (`deleteAutomationRun`) so the recipient
   is NOT treated as "already followed up" on the next run. Terminal blocks
   (opt-out/DNC/consent/invalid number) keep their row and stay final/counted.
   To make this possible, `sendAutomationNow` now additionally returns `gate_rule` and
   `run_id` (additive fields; no behaviour change for `processNewLead`/`tickAutomations`).

3. **Retry once on failure** — a `failed` send is retried ONCE after 20s
   (`FAILED_RETRY_DELAY_MS`); `retried` is reported.

4. **End-of-run summary** — prints `sent`, `blocked` broken down by reason, `failed`,
   `retried`, `skipped`, and the remaining candidate count
   (rate-capped + finally-failed = still owed a message).

Note on the other caller of `hasAutomationRun`: `processNewLead` (immediate `new_lead`
send) has the SAME latent bug — a rate-cap-blocked or failed immediate would leave a
'skipped'/'failed' row that blocks a re-send. Per N5 rule 2 ("if they do, note it in
followups, do not fix"), it is recorded in `defects/finish-plan-followups.md`, not fixed here.
